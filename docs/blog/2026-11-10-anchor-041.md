**0.4.1** is the follow-on to 0.4.0: headless survey flags, critic thinking-on
with a confirmation pass, and parser/prompt fixes so Lightning Verdicts
parse.

<!-- truncate -->

`anchor review --approve` / `--needs-work` / `--skip` / `--defer` consume the
survey from argv (`--force-approve` when the critic is not ACCEPT). The
NVIDIA critic chat uses thinking-on, a short system prompt, and two
independent passes; `--critic-passes 1` skips the extra chat. `## Verdict`
must be a single word; empty `## Verdict` then `## ACCEPT` parses as ACCEPT.
`anchor work --finish` counts commits in the plan's linked worktree.

Hygiene and plan-folders branches are not in this tag.
